# Publishing notes

This repository is a sanitized public copy of the EC156 reverse-engineering
work.  Device-unique identifiers (ESN, MEID, IMSI, hardware revision raw
values) have been replaced with `<redacted-...>` placeholders.  No firmware
image, bootloader, signing material, or flash tooling is included, and no
flash was attempted on the working unit.

Raw proprietary decompilations and vendor CD artifacts are intentionally
omitted; the analysis documents summarize their conclusions.
